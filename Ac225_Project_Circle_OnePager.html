Start Here: Motivation
Step 1: Clinical Target
The need

When attached to a cancer-targeting medicine, Ac-225 releases short-range alpha radiation that can damage cancer cells. It is scarce and difficult to produce.

2
Step 2: Source Isotope
The starting material

The pathway begins with Ra-226, a long-lived radioactive material used as the target that receives the fast neutrons.

3
Step 3: Transmutation
The nuclear pathway

Deuterons strike beryllium and release fast neutrons. The neutrons convert some Ra-226 into Ra-225, which naturally decays into Ac-225.

4
Step 4: Ground Truth
The trusted references

A trusted scientific solver called Radau generated the training examples. An exact Bateman calculation later checked the same simplified equations.

5
Step 5: Physics-Guided AI
The model I built

An LSTM, a model that learns sequences over time, follows the isotope timeline while physics rules discourage impossible answers.

6
Step 6: Synthetic Screening
The 10,000-scenario test

V3 recovered 18 of Radau's top 20 candidates and selected the candidate Radau ranked second, only 0.047% below the best score.

7
Step 7: Real-World Verification
The reality check

A Berkeley test used conditions outside V3's training range and failed. It showed the next model needs better neutron-spectrum, target-geometry, and beam-timing data.

What I Have Completed

  • Built a reduced five-isotope physics model
  • Generated 1,400 training examples with Radau
  • Trained the V3 LSTM for 6,000 epochs
  • Compared it with a similarly sized non-recurrent model
  • Froze the model before new comparisons
  • Audited 10,000 simplified scenarios
  • Checked speed against exact and Radau solvers
  • Preserved the failed Berkeley transfer test

What Must Happen Next

1. Decide exactly what a real researcher needs the model to choose.
2. Obtain realistic neutron, beam-timing, target-geometry, and uncertainty data.
3. Have an expert check the tracked isotopes and impurity goal.
4. Generate richer training examples with trusted nuclear software.
5. Set aside an unseen benchmark before retraining once.
6. Recheck every suggested finalist with trusted software.

Current evidence: On 10,000 simplified computer scenarios, V3 was 84.9 times faster than the general Radau solver but 56.7 times slower than the exact calculation for the same reduced equations. It is a prototype for screening first and verifying second; it has not yet shown that real Ac-225 production is faster, cheaper, or experimentally validated.
