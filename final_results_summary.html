❓ Why did the Flagship PI-LSTM score 5.12% vs. 4.51%? (Explaining the Numbers)

If you look at the raw numbers, you might wonder: "Why does v2 say 4.51% while Flagship PI-LSTM says 5.12%?"

Here is the scientific explanation:

  • 1. Different Scoring Protocols: The 4.51% figure comes from the standalone v2 test set (`v63_validation`). When we put BOTH models on the EXACT same 22-scenario paired benchmark, v2 scores 8.18%, while PI-LSTM scores 5.12%. So PI-LSTM is actually 37% MORE accurate on identical test scenarios!
  • 2. Massive Speed & Impurity Gains: The PI-LSTM model compresses the heavy 12k PINN into a lightweight recurrent architecture that runs at 1.65 ms per scenario (~83× faster) while cutting toxic Ra-227 impurity error by over 54%!
