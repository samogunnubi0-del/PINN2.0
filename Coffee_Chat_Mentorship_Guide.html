Coffee Chat Playbook • Computer Science Mentorship

CS Coffee Chat Cheatsheet & Live Notetaker

Make a strong impression, build a genuine connection, and turn this short coffee break into a lasting mentorship relationship.

☕ High-Impact Questions to Ask 3 Core Stages
1. Backstory & Decisions
"What originally made you choose CS, and looking back, what key decisions or milestones helped you get to where you are today?"
💡 Why ask this: Everyone loves sharing their origin story. This gets him talking about his real turning points (specific classes, early projects, internships, mentors) without feeling grilled.
2. Mindsets & Work Ethic
"As someone who works hard and wants to achieve a similar level of success, what habits, mindsets, or specific skills do you think made the biggest difference for you early on?"
💡 Why ask this: Shows you admire his drive and possess high initiative. You learn the non-obvious habits (daily LeetCode, GitHub streaks, cold emailing, deep focus) that separated him from the crowd.
3. Concrete Recommendations
"What campus resources, organizations, or types of projects do you highly recommend I focus on right now to set myself up for success?"
💡 Why ask this: Yields actionable, localized intel (specific research labs, ACM/hackathons, professors, open-source projects) that you can immediately execute on.
🎯 How to Structure for Long-Term Benefit Strategy
✊🏾

1. Show Genuine Respect for His Work Ethic

Let him know you admire his drive and impact. People are far more willing to mentor someone who shows active initiative and an authentic hunger to learn rather than someone just looking for an easy referral.

🎯

2. Be Specific About Your Goals

Briefly mention what areas of tech or personal growth you are focused on (e.g., software engineering, AI/ML, leadership) so his advice can be tailored to you.

📝

3. Take Quick Notes During / Right After

Mentally note or jot down specific books, tools, or clubs he mentions on the right panel. Mentioning his exact recommendations in your follow-up email proves you actually listened.

🤝

4. Set Up the Next Step (Before Wrapping Up)

Never leave without closing the loop. Use the script below right before getting up from the table:

Closing Script (Say this at minute 25):

"Would it be alright if I stayed in touch on LinkedIn and reached out occasionally as I work on these recommendations?"

✉️ Follow-Up Message (Send Within 24h)

Send this on LinkedIn or Email the next morning. Reference something specific he recommended to stand out:

Hi [Name], Thank you so much for taking the time to grab coffee with me today. I really appreciated hearing about your journey in CS and your advice regarding [mention 1 specific thing, e.g., building portfolio projects / joining ACM]. I’m already looking into [specific tool/club he suggested]. I’d love to stay in touch as I make progress. Thanks again for your time and guidance! Best regards, [Your Name]
