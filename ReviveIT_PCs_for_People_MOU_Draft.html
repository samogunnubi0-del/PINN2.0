ReviveIT

A student-led outreach initiative founded at Glen Burnie High School

Memorandum of Understanding

Discussion draft for review — not a final agreement

Date: August 12, 2026  ·  Parties: ReviveIT (Samuel Ogunnubi and Mit Patel, Co-Founders) and PCs for People, Maryland
Re: Volunteer outreach partnership for private-school technology referrals

1. Purpose

ReviveIT is a student-led initiative that identifies private and independent schools and other organizations with unused technology and introduces authorized donor representatives to PCs for People for secure pickup, data sanitization, reuse, and recycling. This volunteer partnership gives PCs for People qualified community leads while giving participating students verified service, leadership experience, and appropriately attributed impact.

2. ReviveIT will

  • Research and contact appropriate decision-makers using approved information
  • Confirm basic lead details: contact, equipment estimate, and expected timing
  • Introduce interested donors to PCs for People's designated Maryland contact
  • Keep an accurate referral and volunteer-hour log
  • Use PCs for People's name, logo, and claims only as approved in writing

Students will never wipe, repair, transport, store, sell, or take custody of devices or data.

3. PCs for People will (operations)

  • Provide a Maryland contact and an approved referral/handoff process
  • Assess eligibility, fees, timing, access, and pickup requirements directly with each donor
  • Handle ownership forms, pickup, processing, data sanitization, and recycling
  • Aim to acknowledge warm leads within five business days
  • Give ReviveIT current talking points and accepted-item information

4. Student recognition and partnership support

  • Approved descriptor: student-led community outreach partner supporting PCs for People Maryland
  • A brief partnership confirmation and approved name/logo guidance
  • ReviveIT-sourced referrals tagged when operationally possible
  • Available semester totals for completed ReviveIT referrals
  • Verification of documented hours, responsibilities, leadership, and results
  • After a successful year or agreed milestone, one brief college/scholarship support letter for each founding student director upon request; wording and signer remain at PCs for People's discretion
  • One orientation and, if available, one facility visit or volunteer day
  • Public acknowledgment after a successful pilot, subject to approval

5. Custody and limits

  • Each donor confirms ownership and authorizes transfer directly to PCs for People.
  • ReviveIT members are volunteers, not employees or agents, and cannot promise pickup or accept liability.
  • ReviveIT does not represent or bind Glen Burnie High School or AACPS without written authorization.
  • This MOU is nonexclusive and records shared intentions rather than creating payment obligations.

6. Goal and term

  • Proposed year-one target: 3–5 completed pickups or equivalent meaningful volume, reviewed after the first pickup and not a quota or guarantee.
  • Through 2026–27; renews yearly unless ended with 30 days’ written notice.
  • Either party may end name/logo use immediately if inaccurate or harmful.

This discussion draft may be revised or replaced with PCs for People's standard language while preserving the agreed outreach, attribution, student-verification, and operating principles above.
