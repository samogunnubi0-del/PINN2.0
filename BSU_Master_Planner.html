2 Field Trips Strategy & Maryland/DC HBCU Outreach

To maximize student college exposure, BSU is staging two distinct expeditions this school year: a Fall Maryland HBCU tour and a February Black History Month Washington, DC collegiate & museum expedition.

Expedition #1 • Late Fall (Dec 4, 2026)

Maryland HBCU Immersion Tour

Destination: Bowie State University (Bowie, MD) or Morgan State University (Baltimore, MD).
Itinerary: Undergraduate admissions presentation, student-led walking campus tour (dorms, student center, labs), dining hall lunch, and department meetings.

Expedition #2 • Black History Month (Feb 19, 2027)

Washington, DC Collegiate & Cultural Tour

Destination: Howard University ("The Mecca") + Smithsonian National Museum of African American History & Culture (NMAAHC).
Itinerary: Morning tour of Howard University campus → Georgia Avenue lunch → Afternoon exploration of Smithsonian NMAAHC on the National Mall.

Dues Framework & Official Parent Notice

A clear, defensible dues policy ensures transparency with school administration, parents, and incoming students.

Approved 2026–2027 Membership Tiers Official Policy
Tier 1: Returning Member Renewal $15.00 / year

For returning BSU members: Covers full active membership registration, refreshments at all bi-weekly meetings, access to college fair prep materials, study jams, and subsidized field trip rates. (Uses existing club shirt/gear).

Tier 2: New Member Full Package (Includes T-Shirt) $25.00 / year

For freshmen & incoming new members: Includes full membership registration, all meeting refreshments PLUS the custom screen-printed 2026–2027 Black Student Union club t-shirt and priority seat reservation for the 2 college tours. (Also available for returning members who want the new shirt design).

Tier 3: Confidential Fee Assistance (Waiver) $0.00

Financial Inclusion Policy: No student will ever be excluded from BSU activities, meetings, or field trips due to financial hardship. Full confidential waivers are sponsored by club fundraising.

Where Does Every Dollar Go?

  • 40% Bus & Field Trip Subsidy: Offsets charter coach booking deposits so college tours cost students minimal out-of-pocket ($15 seat fee).
  • 30% Refreshments & Meeting Food: Pizza, drinks, chips, and snacks for all bi-weekly meetings to support retention and community.
  • 20% Club Materials & Cords: College Fair prep kits, poster supplies, and authentic graduation Kente stoles/cords for active seniors.
  • 10% Discretionary Fee Assistance: Offsets costs for members experiencing temporary financial hardship.

November College Fair Readiness Kit

Structured preparation protocol so BSU students arrive confident, polished, and ready to secure admission connections and fee waivers.

Student Checklist (For Fair Day) Prep Standard

☑️ 10 Printed Resumes: Include GPA, honors, BSU leadership, athletics, and academic interests.

☑️ Professional Email: Use firstname.lastname@gmail.com rather than school or informal handles.

☑️ Dedicated Notebook & Pen: For logging representative names, application codes, and deadlines.

☑️ Target Institution List: Research 5–8 specific colleges prior to arrival.

☑️ Business-Casual Attire: Collared shirt, blouse, polo, or clean school spirit wear.

4 High-Impact Questions for Admissions Reps Talking Points

1. Merit Aid & Priority Deadlines:
"What institutional merit scholarships or diversity leadership awards are available for incoming freshmen, and what are the strict deadlines?"

2. Black Student Support & Community:
"What does the Black Student Union or multicultural student support network look like on your campus?"

3. Application Fee Waivers:
"Do you have an application fee waiver code available for high school students attending today's fair?"

4. Follow-Up Business Card:
"May I have your business card or direct email so I can follow up when I submit my application?"
