1 | CLINICAL GOAL & NUCLEAR DEPLETION CHALLENGE
The $0.9B+ TAT Crisis: Actinium-225 (225Ac) is a breakthrough alpha-emitter for targeted cancer therapies (e.g., PSMA-617 prostate trials). Global supply meets <3% of clinical demand (~2 Ci/yr produced vs 50+ Ci/yr projected), severely bottlenecking patient access.
The Fast-Neutron Route: Irradiating 226Ra with fast neutrons (33/40 MeV d+Be) via 226Ra(n,2n)225Ra → 225Ac (t1/2=9.92d) offers a scalable accelerator alternative without requiring scarce Th-229 parent generators.
The Mathematical Wall (Stiffness): Competing thermal (n,γ) captures produce toxic 227Ac via 227Ra (t1/2=42.2 min). Coupling 1,600-year 226Ra decay with 42.2-minute 227Ra creates a stiffness ratio exceeding 20,000,000 : 1.
Why Traditional Solvers Fail: Implicit Runge-Kutta (Radau5) solvers must take microscopic time-steps to resolve fast transients. Evaluating 10,000+ candidate multi-flux schedules for harvest optimization requires hours of compute, prohibiting digital twins.
3 | VERIFIED RESULTS & MULTI-OBJECTIVE SCREENING
High-Fidelity Accuracy: V3 PI-LSTM achieved 0.97% median relative error across matched multi-isotope test scenarios (vs 2.91% for parameter-matched MLP). V4 finite-geometry proxy benchmark scored 1.83% median error across 5 independent seeds.
84.9x Computational Speedup: Inference takes ~2-4 ms per full multi-week irradiation and decay trajectory (compared to ~200-400 ms for adaptive Radau5). Allows instantaneous screening of vast combinatorial parameter spaces.
Combinatorial Schedule Optimization: Screened 10,000 candidate beam-flux, duration, and cooling schedules. The surrogate's top-ranked schedule landed within 0.047% of Radau's best score (Rank 2 of 10,000), pinpointing the exact optimal harvest window.
100% OOD Safety Rejection: When probed with unmodeled neutron flux magnitudes or energies, the conformal safety gate successfully warned on 184 of 184 out-of-domain cases, preventing silent surrogate failure.
2 | PHYSICS-INFORMED SURROGATE ARCHITECTURE
1. Integrated Trapezoidal Loss: Standard PINNs penalize derivative residuals (dN/dt), amplifying stiff numerical noise. I formulated an integrated mass-balance loss: N(t) = N(0) + ∫A·N dt. This mathematical smoothing eliminated stiffness overshoots entirely.
2. Hard Initial Condition Ansatz: Constructed the neural output layer to enforce N(t=0) = N0 strictly by definition. Prevents unphysical mass creation, negative isotope densities, or spontaneous atom hallucination at early timesteps.
3. Multiscale Fourier Encodings: Embedded time and incident neutron energy into sinusoidal basis functions [sin(2kπx), cos(2kπx)]. Overcomes neural spectral bias to capture the sharp 6.42 MeV (n,2n) reaction threshold and rapid decay transients.
4. Guaranteed Uncertainty & OOD Gate: Replaced unphysical MC Dropout with Conformal Prediction for mathematically calibrated 95% error bands. Added Mahalanobis distance gating to detect and safely reject out-of-distribution inputs.
