Mission & Purpose

What is BSU & What Do We Do?

At the end of the day, we wanted this space to grow as a community and support each other in Black culture.

Our Heart & Mission

Highlighting Black Excellence

BSU is where we want to highlight Black excellence. High school can be stressful and divided—this room is built as a genuine community where we uplift one another, share our cultural pride, and hold each other to high standards.

Whether you’re a freshman or a senior, you have a support network here that will push you to succeed.

Real Action & Activities

Experiences That Matter

• College Field Trips: Touring HBCUs & Black historical museums.
• Annual HBCU College Fair: Highlighting historic institutions for our upcoming juniors.
• Spirit Week & Potluck: Celebrating Black History Month with school-wide events and community feasting.
• Study Jams & Game Nights: Finals prep with hot cocoa and zero stress.

Club Culture & Vibe

The Real Vibe: We Have Fun & Play Games!

Apologies for the "information bomb" at today's first meeting—we had to get all the business out of the way!

🎮 What Everyday BSU Meetings Actually Look Like:

• It’s Chill: This is NOT another lecture or test. You come in, grab snacks, sit down, and breathe.
• Games & Fun: We play Black history trivia, Uno, board games, video games, and music battles.
• Real Conversations: We talk about life, culture, college, and what's going on in school.
• Zero Pressure: Whether you want to lead or just come hang out and eat snacks, you belong here!
