    Pacing Iron Laws for 700+
    • The 90-Second Cap: If a reading passage takes $>90$ seconds, pick an educated guess, flag it, and move forward immediately. Protect easy points.
    • Grammar First: Grammar and Transitions (Q16–27) have zero ambiguity. Clear them first when energy is at 100%.
    • Never Trust Your Ear: "Reading by sound" causes 80% of grammar errors on the SAT. Apply the Boolean rules mechanically.
    • No Blanks Ever: There is no guessing penalty on the Digital SAT.
    Module A: Boolean Grammar Engine (Deterministic $T/F$)
    Because your Standard English Conventions is currently in the 370–410 band, we replace subjective "ear listening" with mathematical clause logic.
    Rule 1: Indep + Indep
    Indep + Indep ⇒ Semicolon (;) | Period (.) | Comma + FANBOYS

    Two complete sentences can NEVER be joined by a lone comma (comma splice) or nothing (run-on).

    Rule 2: Indep + Dep
    Indep + Dep ⇒ Comma (,) | No Punctuation

    A dependent clause (starts with although, because, since, which) cannot take a semicolon or period.

    Rule 3: List / Explanation
    Indep + List/Spec ⇒ Colon (:) | Single Dash (—)

    A colon or single dash MUST have a complete Independent Clause before it.
