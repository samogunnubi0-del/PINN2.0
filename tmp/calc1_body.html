    Set which week your class is on. Your job is the next week, not last week’s panic. Math Sorcerer’s playlist uses Larson-style numbering, which is what most Calculus & Analytic Geometry 1 courses use.

    Playlist honesty
    • 1Videos 1–10 are book and study advice. Required: “The Best Way to Learn Calculus” and “Calculus For Beginners.”
    • 2Lectures start at 1.2. There is no 1.3 (limit laws) in this playlist. Use the Khan gap card or you will freeze on 0/0 limits.
    • 3After Newton’s method the playlist jumps to ln/exp. Chapter 4 (Riemann sums, FTC, u-sub) is on Khan. That is usually a midterm or the final.
    • 4Analytic geometry (distance, midpoint, circles, lines) is assumed, not taught. Formula gym covers it so class does not “end you.”
