Personal note forDr. John BrockmanUniversity of Missouri Research Reactor

My name is Samuel Ogunnubi, and I am a 12th grader in Glen Burnie High School's Biomedical and Allied Health program in Maryland. I started this project after learning what Ac-225 can do in targeted cancer treatment. My goal was to see whether a computer model could help compare production plans faster.

I built a physics-guided LSTM around irradiation, isotope buildup and decay, and cooling. Then I found that an exact solver is already faster for my small constant-rate equation set. If the project is going to matter, it has to study the real end-to-end timeline and the constraints that actually slow a facility down.
