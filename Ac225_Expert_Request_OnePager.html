Current Technical Contract

Frozen model and pathway

  • Ra-226(n,2n)Ra-225 to Ac-225, with Ra-226(n,gamma)Ra-227 to Ac-227 represented as a competing branch.
  • Physics-informed LSTM, reduced five-nuclide system, 1,400 Radau-generated training scenarios, 6,000 epochs, seed 42.
  • Constant irradiation schedule followed by exact decay-only cooling; activity reported per 1 g initial Ra-226.
  • Frozen checkpoint and source hashes are preserved. No tuning is allowed after outside results are seen.

Post-lock reality checks

  • The LSTM beat a parameter-matched MLP on pointwise error, but the MLP ranked the full grid more accurately and ran faster.
  • Berkeley 33 and 40 MeV cases were outside the training domain; raw V3 activity predictions were about 600 times too high.
  • The reduced constant-rate equations have an exact analytic solution that is faster and more accurate than V3.
  • Current value is limited to screening candidates that are then checked with a trusted solver.
Claim boundary: The project does not yet demonstrate reactor accuracy, experimental validation, a universal error below 3%, clinical readiness, production cost savings, or a decisive LSTM advantage.
